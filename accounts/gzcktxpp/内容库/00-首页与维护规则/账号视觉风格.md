# Account Visual Style（账号视觉风格）｜广州出口退税-翩翩

> Account ID: `gzcktxpp`
>
> This file defines the long-term visual DNA for spoken supporting visuals.
>
> 本文件只定义“这个账号怎么看起来”，不定义“这条内容讲什么”。

---

## 1. Visual Positioning（视觉定位）

```yaml
visual_identity:
  core_impression: "冷静判断型出口退税视觉：专业、现代、利落，重点明确，结论有力量但不把专业感绑定到固定UI结构"
  audience_feeling: "像一个经验成熟的出口退税顾问，能快速抓住当前业务最重要的判断点，并用清楚、现代的视觉把它讲明白"
  differentiation_goal: "以海运青色系、强信息层级、冷静判断气质和高完成度形成稳定辨识度"
```

Visual principle:

> **先让最重要的判断一眼可见，再选择最适合当前业务语义的视觉表达。**

The account Visual DNA defines tone, color, hierarchy, and finish. It MUST NOT force SaaS cards, dashboards, status modules, documents, scenes, or relationship diagrams as permanent defaults.

---

## 2. Rendering Language（渲染语言）

```yaml
rendering:
  primary: "polished modern business visual with content-adaptive information design, schematic, editorial, object, or selective realistic rendering"
  secondary: "clean 2D information design, business objects, restrained realistic support, or visual modules chosen by page meaning"
  finish: "high-end business presentation, crisp hierarchy, restrained depth, modern international business feel"
  realism_level: "content-adaptive; clarity and professional judgment above a fixed rendering technique"
```

The default foundation is a complete professional visual page with strong hierarchy, disciplined spacing, refined typography, and controlled whitespace.

Cards, status labels, comparisons, checklists, timelines, real export-business objects, documents, scenes, and relationship structures are all optional tools. Choose them only when they improve the current page's viewer-understanding target.

Do not use any one of these techniques as the account's permanent visual signature.

## 3. Color System（色彩系统）

```yaml
color:
  primary_colors:
    - "海运青 #147A82"
    - "冷青蓝 #43A8B2"
  secondary_colors:
    - "深海蓝灰 #17343C"
    - "冷白浅青灰 #F3F8F8"
  accent_colors:
    - "珊瑚橙 #F26B4A"
  warning_colors:
    - "深珊瑚橙，仅用于高风险状态"
  background_preference: "冷白、浅青灰或深海蓝灰，可按当前内容选择"
  saturation: "中低饱和，异常节点可中高饱和"
  contrast: "中高对比"
```

Color association:
- cyan / teal = export / international business identity;
- coral / orange = key judgment, break point, important result, or money node.

Avoid generic tax-office blue.

## 4. Typography（字体与文字层级）

```yaml
typography:
  family: "modern Simplified Chinese sans-serif"
  title: "heavy, decisive, concise"
  conclusion: "large and high-contrast"
  supporting_copy: "medium weight, short lines"
  labels: "compact and highly legible"
  alignment: "grid-aligned; cover may be centered or editorially asymmetric"
```

Avoid decorative, handwritten, rounded-cartoon, glowing, outlined, or mixed-family type.

Typography should communicate decisiveness without forcing every page into a system-interface aesthetic.

```yaml
graphic_language:

  status_labels: "状态语义存在时可用青色或珊瑚橙标签，不作为默认装饰"

  highlights: "大结论、关键判断、色块或单一关键数字"
  separators: "使用留白、对齐、细线或轻色块组织页面，也允许其他清晰分隔方式"
```

Do not add random charts, fake data, meaningless interface decoration, or fabricated official screenshots.

UI is one optional expression layer, not the account identity itself.

## 7. Business Semantics（业务语义表现）

```yaml
business_semantics:
  application: "申报、资料、业务对象、状态或其他形式均按当前语义选择"
  acceptance: "需要表达受理时可用状态、文件、流程或结果型视觉"
  verification: "核查可通过检查、对比、关系、对象或局部细节表达"
  waiting: "等待可通过时间、状态、停滞、资金或业务场景表达"
  completion: "完成可通过结果、状态、资金、对象或其他清晰语义表达"
  cycle: "周期可通过大数字、时间线、时钟或其他直观方式表达"
  diagnosis: "判断可通过结论、对比、分支、关系、场景或对象聚焦表达"
```

Business semantics determine the visual form. Do not hard-code every export-tax-refund concept into a status card or dashboard.

Do not reproduce or fabricate official system interfaces, official notices, banking records, or unreadable pseudo-text. Realistic or semi-realistic business objects may be used when they improve understanding, but must remain generic, accurate in role, and visually subordinate to the confirmed business meaning.

## 8. Human Presence（人物使用）

```yaml
human_presence:
  default_use: "selective"
  exception: "人物动作、角色或场景能明显提升当前业务理解时使用"
  maximum_role: "通常不超过画面信息权重的15%，除非人物本身是页面确认语义的必要主体"
  avoid:
    - "无信息价值的双人讨论"
    - "老板和财务泛化开会"
    - "商务人物摆拍"
    - "夸张焦虑"
    - "税务审讯感"
```

Do not ban people by default. Do not use them as generic decoration either.

```yaml
information_density:
```

Professional quality comes from hierarchy, clarity, and finish. Do not fill empty space with extra cards, arrows, icons, labels, documents, or scenes.

## 12. 3:4 Video Cover Signature（3:4视频封面特征）

```yaml
video_cover:
  role: "微信视频号列表与搜索路径获客入口"
  ratio: "3:4"
  headline: "一个强主标题，优先呈现老板最关心的问题、结论、周期、风险或利益点"
  supporting_hook: "可选一个短辅助钩子，不与主标题争抢"
  focal_strategy: "大字标题 + 一个与当前内容最相关的视觉焦点，可是状态、数字、对象、对比或场景"
  thumbnail_readability: "缩小后仍能在一秒内识别核心信息"
  visual_strength: "比16:9内容页更强对比、更集中，但保持专业克制"
  continuity: "沿用海运青、冷青蓝、珊瑚橙、现代无衬线字体与冷静判断气质"
```

The cover must be recomposed for the vertical frame. Do not crop a 16:9 slide. Avoid sensational clickbait, unsupported guarantees, fake outcomes, unrelated decorative scenery, excessive copy, and more than two competing focal points.

## 17. Completion Check（完成检查）

- Does the page look complete, polished, and professionally resolved?
- Is the main judgment, conclusion, or business point visible within one second?
- Does the page retain the maritime-teal and coral account identity?
- Are layout, typography, visual objects, modules, and whitespace aesthetically resolved?
- Has the design chosen its objects and page structure from the current content instead of a fixed SaaS/dashboard template?
- Are realistic business objects, documents, scenes, or relationship structures allowed when they materially improve understanding?
- Does the deck feel coherent without forcing every page into the same composition?
- Is the 3:4 cover readable at thumbnail size and acquisition-focused without clickbait?

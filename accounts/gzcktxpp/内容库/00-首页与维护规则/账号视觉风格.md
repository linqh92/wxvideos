# Account Visual Style（账号视觉风格）｜广州出口退税-翩翩

> Account ID: `gzcktxpp`
>
> This file defines the long-term visual DNA for spoken supporting visuals.
>
> 本文件只定义“这个账号怎么看起来”，不定义“这条内容讲什么”。

---

## 1. Visual Positioning（视觉定位）

```yaml
visual_identity:
  core_impression: "出口退税专业决策台：清晰、冷静、现代，能迅速给出状态、判断和下一步"
  audience_feeling: "像经验成熟的出口退税顾问，把复杂问题整理成老板一眼能看懂的专业PPT"
  differentiation_goal: "以海运青色系、利落信息层级和状态判断感形成稳定辨识度"
```

Visual principle:

> **先让结论一眼可见，再用专业信息模块解释判断依据。**

---

## 2. Rendering Language（渲染语言）

```yaml
rendering:
  primary: "polished enterprise PPT information design"
  secondary: "clean SaaS-style status, comparison, checklist, timeline, and decision modules"
  finish: "high-end business presentation, crisp 2D interface graphics, restrained depth"
  realism_level: "low; simplified professional icons and neutral schematic objects only when needed"
```

The default foundation is a complete PPT page with clear typography hierarchy, disciplined grid alignment, refined cards and status modules, restrained shadows, and controlled whitespace.

Do not use realistic export goods, ports, containers, semi-realistic documents, or chain diagrams as the account's default visual signature.

---

## 3. Color System（色彩系统）

```yaml
color:
  primary_colors:
    - "海运青 #147A82"
    - "冷青蓝 #43A8B2"
  secondary_colors:
    - "深海蓝灰 #17343C"
    - "冷白浅青灰 #F3F8F8"
  accent_colors:
    - "珊瑚橙 #F26B4A"
  warning_colors:
    - "深珊瑚橙，仅用于高风险状态"
  background_preference: "冷白、浅青灰或深海蓝灰"
  saturation: "中低饱和，异常节点可中高饱和"
  contrast: "中高对比"
```

Color association:
- cyan / teal = export / logistics / international trade;
- coral / orange = diagnosis / break point / important money node.

Avoid generic tax-office blue.

---

## 4. Typography（字体与文字层级）

```yaml
typography:
  family: "modern Simplified Chinese sans-serif"
  title: "heavy, decisive, concise"
  conclusion: "large and high-contrast"
  supporting_copy: "medium weight, short lines"
  labels: "compact and highly legible"
  alignment: "grid-aligned; cover may be centered or editorially asymmetric"
```

Avoid decorative, handwritten, rounded-cartoon, glowing, outlined, or mixed-family type.

---

## 5. UI and Graphic Language（UI与图形语言）

```yaml
graphic_language:
  cards: "冷白或浅青卡片，统一圆角、冷灰细边和克制阴影"
  status_labels: "青色系状态标签，等待或关键判断使用珊瑚橙"

  separators: "使用留白、对齐、细线和轻色块组织页面"

  depth: "以清晰2D为主，仅保留轻微层次"
```

Do not add random charts, fake data, meaningless interface decoration, or fabricated official screenshots.

## 9. Information Density（信息密度）

```yaml
information_density:
  level: "medium-low"
  main_message: 1
  supporting_points: "1–3"
  primary_modules: 1
  visible_labels: "2–5个"
  visual_scan_target: "1 second"
```

Professional quality comes from hierarchy and restraint. Do not fill empty space with extra cards, arrows, icons, or labels.

## 12. 3:4 Video Cover Signature（3:4视频封面特征）

```yaml
video_cover:
  role: "微信视频号列表与搜索路径获客入口"
  ratio: "3:4"
  headline: "一个强主标题，优先呈现老板最关心的问题、结论、周期、风险或利益点"
  supporting_hook: "可选一个短辅助钩子，不与主标题争抢"
  focal_strategy: "大字标题 + 单一状态冲突、关键数字或判断提示"
  thumbnail_readability: "缩小后仍能在一秒内识别核心信息"
  visual_strength: "比16:9内容页更强对比、更集中，但保持专业克制"
  continuity: "沿用海运青、冷青蓝、珊瑚橙、现代无衬线字体与克制UI语言"
```

The cover must be recomposed for the vertical frame. Do not crop a 16:9 slide. Avoid sensational clickbait, unsupported guarantees, fake outcomes, unrelated export scenery, excessive copy, and more than two competing focal points.

## 17. Completion Check（完成检查）

- Does the page look like a complete professional PPT slide?
- Is the main conclusion or status visible within one second?
- Does the page retain the maritime-teal and coral account identity?
- Are layout, typography, cards, icons, and whitespace aesthetically resolved?
- Has the design avoided realistic export objects, semi-realistic documents, and chain-diagnosis diagrams as default logic?
- Does the deck feel coherent without forcing every page into the same composition?
- Is the 3:4 cover readable at thumbnail size and acquisition-focused without clickbait?
- Does the cover use stronger emphasis while remaining visually continuous with the 16:9 content deck?

# Account Visual Style（账号视觉风格）｜广州敏哥聊财税

> Account ID: `gzminge`
>
> This file defines the long-term visual DNA for spoken supporting visuals.
>
> 本文件只定义“这个账号怎么看起来”，不定义“这条内容讲什么”。

---

## 1. Visual Positioning（视觉定位）

```yaml
visual_identity:
  core_impression: "成熟直接的经营型财税视觉：懂生意、懂钱、重结果，判断利落但不过度技术化"
  audience_feeling: "像一个真正理解老板经营动作和结果的人，把复杂财税问题快速讲到最影响生意的那一层"
  differentiation_goal: "区别于传统财税宣传、轻创业教程和高冷咨询报告；形成成熟、直接、商业感强的视觉气质"
```

Visual principle:

> **先看经营结果，再选择最适合当前内容的视觉表达。**

The account should visually communicate:
- owner-side judgment;
- operating consequence;
- commercial maturity;
- direct information hierarchy;
- practical confidence without visual overengineering.

Account Visual DNA controls presentation tone, not the required business objects or page archetype.

---

## 2. Rendering Language（渲染语言）

```yaml
rendering:
  primary: "mature modern business visual with content-adaptive 2D, editorial, schematic, or selective realistic support"
  secondary: "clean business information design with restrained depth and selective object realism when semantically useful"
  realism_level: "content-adaptive; clarity and commercial maturity above a fixed rendering technique"
  allowed_hybrid:
    - "真实经营对象 + 简洁信息层"
    - "平面信息设计 + 必要业务物件"
    - "数字、关系、场景或对象按当前内容自由组合"
```

Default visual behavior:
- choose the rendering method from the current page meaning first;
- dashboards, data cards, accounts, invoices, objects, scenes, comparisons, and diagrams are optional tools, not account defaults;
- business objects may be realistic, schematic, editorial, or icon-based when that improves comprehension;
- preserve a mature commercial finish regardless of the chosen visual form.

Avoid:
- traditional accounting-office stock photography;
- generic finance brochures;
- childish illustration treatment;
- heavy government-service aesthetics.

---

## 3. Color System（色彩系统）

```yaml
color:
  primary_colors:
    - "深石墨灰"
    - "炭黑灰"
  secondary_colors:
    - "冷灰"
    - "低饱和米白"
  accent_colors:
    - "暖橙"
    - "琥珀黄"
  warning_colors:
    - "高饱和橙红，仅用于真正异常节点"
  background_preference: "深色或中深色为主，可根据当前内容使用浅灰或低饱和米白形成反差"
  saturation: "整体低饱和，强调色中高饱和"
  contrast: "中高对比"
```

Color logic:
- graphite / charcoal = mature business judgment;
- amber / orange = action, result, money, or the current key variable;
- warning color should be used only for true abnormality;
- avoid using blue-gray as the permanent brand base.

---

## 4. Graphic Language（图形语言）

```yaml
graphic_language:
  relationship_lines: "简洁、方向明确；仅在关系本身需要解释时使用"
  arrows: "克制、清楚，不把箭头作为固定装饰"
  cards: "可使用清晰商务卡片，但卡片不是账号默认视觉主体"
  labels: "短标签，优先业务语言和经营结果"
  icons: "简洁几何业务图标，风格成熟，不用可爱插画图标"
  highlights: "暖橙/琥珀局部高亮，可用于关键数字、结果、动作或异常"
  borders: "低对比细边框，按当前页面结构决定是否使用"
  shadows: "轻微层级阴影，不做玻璃拟态炫技"
  depth: "克制层次感；平面、轻透视或真实物件均以理解效率为先"
```

Recurring business vocabulary may include:
- platform order;
- payment;
- company / personal account;
- invoice;
- supplier;
- profit / margin;
- cash flow;
- revenue;
- upstream / downstream.

These are content vocabulary, not mandatory visual motifs.

## 5. Composition（构图倾向）

```yaml
composition:
  preferred_layouts:
    - "强结果焦点 + 必要支持信息"
    - "左右或前后对比"
    - "单一经营问题的清晰结构"
    - "内容需要时使用关系、流程、场景或对象聚焦"
  whitespace: "中等留白"
  focal_strategy: "第一视觉焦点优先落在最影响经营理解的结果、数字、动作或关键关系上"
  symmetry: "可平衡也可非对称，保持直接和有方向感"
  perspective: "正视、轻透视、编辑式物件或场景均可按内容选择"
  camera_language: "稳定、成熟、商业化；不固定为信息图或经营后台"
```

Do not use:
- conference-room compositions as a generic fallback;
- two-person face-to-face shots with no explanatory value;
- decorative desktop scenes with no information value.

---

## 6. Business Object Style（业务对象表现）

```yaml
business_objects:
  documents: "需要时可用真实感、简化卡片或局部字段表现；不默认固定形式"
  accounts: "仅在账户主体或资金归属影响当前判断时重点出现"
  money_flow: "仅在收付款关系重要时使用；金额可成为视觉焦点但不是强制"
  goods_flow: "电商/跨境等内容需要时自由使用，可作为主视觉或辅助对象"
  process_nodes: "流程存在时使用，避免把非流程内容硬画成节点链"
  risk_markers: "橙红只标记真实风险，不承担装饰功能"
  check_markers: "用于明确匹配、完成或核对语义，不作为固定账号符号"
```

Business objects are selected from the current content semantics. The Visual DNA must not force accounts, invoices, dashboards, or money-flow diagrams into unrelated pages.

---

## 7. Human Presence（人物使用）

```yaml
human_presence:
  default_use: "selective"
  maximum_role: "通常不超过画面信息权重的20%，除非人物本身是当前业务语义不可替代的主体"
  preferred_view: "手部、侧身、局部人物或完整人物均可按内容决定"
  preferred_action: "经营、查看订单、核对资料、付款收款、判断业务等真正帮助理解的动作"
  avoid:
    - "无信息价值的双人对话"
    - "泛商务握手"
    - "夸张皱眉/惊讶"
    - "普通职场摆拍"
```

Use people when their presence improves understanding or commercial realism. Do not remove them solely to preserve a dashboard-like look.

---

## 8. Information Density（信息密度）

```yaml
information_density:
  level: "medium-high"
  max_primary_relations: 1
  max_process_steps: 5
  max_labels: "4–7个短标签"
  visual_scan_target: "1 second"
```

The account can carry relatively rich business information, but:
- one image still explains one main judgment;
- data density must come from the content, not from a desire to look analytical;
- only show information that changes the owner's understanding.

## 9. Text Usage（图内文字）

```yaml
text_usage:
  chinese_text_level: "low-medium"
  short_labels: "允许账户、平台、供应商、收入、成本、利润等业务短标签"
  numbers: "关键金额、比例、利润、流水差异等可在确有业务意义时成为视觉焦点"
  typography_feel: "现代、利落、成熟、偏商业经营感"
```

Exact Chinese sentences should remain concise. Do not force every page into a numeric or data-heavy typography style.

## 10. Preferred Visual Forms（优先示意形式）

```yaml

  - "comparison"
  - "relationship"
  - "process"
  - "object_focus"
  - "risk_highlight"
  - "reconciliation"
  - "real_scene_support"
```

Selection rule:
- choose the form from the current page's viewer-understanding target;
- no item above is mandatory or permanently preferred across all content;
- dashboards and data modules are optional, not the account's defining structure.

## 11. Style-Specific Negative Rules（账号风格反向词）

```yaml
style_negative:
  avoid_rendering:
    - "卡通儿童风"
    - "甜美插画风"
    - "传统蓝白政务宣传风"
    - "纯写实办公室人物摆拍作为默认解法"
  avoid_colors:
    - "大面积标准税务蓝"
    - "粉色甜系"
    - "彩虹多色"
  avoid_layouts:
    - "固定经营Dashboard模板"
    - "满屏小卡片或无意义数据组件"
    - "所有内容都强行画成账户/流水关系"
  avoid_materials:
    - "廉价塑料3D"
    - "过度玻璃拟态"
  avoid_effects:
    - "霓虹赛博朋克"
    - "复杂HUD"
    - "夸张红色警报"
  avoid_people_style:
    - "夸张职场表演"
    - "无信息价值的双人讨论"
  other:
    - "泛财税宣传海报感"
    - "无业务语义的抽象科技背景"
```

## 12. Cross-Account Differentiation（跨账号差异要求）

```yaml
cross_account_differentiation:
  unique_color_signature: "深石墨/炭灰 + 暖橙/琥珀"
  unique_rendering_signature: "成熟、直接、商业经营感；不绑定某一种图形技术"
  unique_graphic_signature: "暖橙强调关键结果，整体利落克制"
  unique_composition_signature: "结果优先、商业重点明确、信息节奏直接"
```

The visual identity should feel closer to:
> **成熟老板视角 / 经营结果判断**
than:
> 财税宣传页 / 初创教程 / 固定数据后台模板.

## 13. Visual Flexibility Boundary（风格弹性边界）

```yaml
flexibility:
  can_change:
    - "页面原型"
    - "是否出现真实物件或真实场景"
    - "卡片、关系图、流程图、数字模块的使用比例"
    - "人物是否出现"
    - "浅色或深色构图"
  must_remain_stable:
    - "深石墨+暖橙的色彩逻辑"
    - "成熟直接的商业气质"
    - "经营结果优先的信息节奏"
    - "中高信息承载能力"
    - "利落、低噪音的完成度"
```

---

## 14. Priority Rule（优先级）

```text
信息准确
>
当前经营问题
>
最适合当前页面语义的视觉解释形式
>
账号成熟直接视觉DNA
>
装饰效果
```

If a dashboard, relationship map, realistic scene, or any other visual technique makes the current topic harder to understand, choose another form.

---

## 15. Completion Check（完成检查）

- Visual first impression feels mature, direct, and commercially aware.
- The main business result or judgment is easy to identify.
- The page does not rely on dashboards, accounts, or data cards merely to signal professionalism.
- The style remains distinct from gzxzcs's younger and lighter tone and qycslc's calmer long-term decision tone.
- Human presence is chosen by content rather than prohibited by style.
- Orange/amber is used for result, action, or emphasis, not decoration.

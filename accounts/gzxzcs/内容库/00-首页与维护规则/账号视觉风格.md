# Account Visual Style（账号视觉风格）｜广州小张说财税

> Account ID: `gzxzcs`
>
> This file defines the long-term visual DNA for spoken supporting visuals.
>
> 本文件只定义“这个账号怎么看起来”，不定义“这条内容讲什么”。

---

## 1. Visual Positioning（视觉定位）

```yaml
visual_identity:
  core_impression: "年轻亲和的基础财税视觉：明快、好懂、低门槛，让第一次创业的人不被专业感挡在外面"
  audience_feeling: "像一个懂行又好沟通的人，把复杂工商财税问题讲得轻松、清楚，而且知道用户现在最关心什么"
  differentiation_goal: "区别于成熟经营诊断、长期战略咨询和冷静专业判断视觉；更年轻、更亲和、更轻盈"
```

Visual principle:

> **先让第一次创业的人愿意看、看得懂，再谈专业。**

The visual should feel:
- approachable;
- clear;
- practical;
- lively but not childish;
- modern and friendly without becoming a fixed tutorial template.

Account Visual DNA controls tone and finish, not a mandatory 2.5D, card, or step-by-step structure.

---

## 2. Rendering Language（渲染语言）

```yaml
rendering:
  primary: "light modern business visual with content-adaptive editorial, schematic, object, or selective realistic rendering"
  secondary: "clean illustration, simple 2D/2.5D objects, real-object support, or concise business scene when useful"
  realism_level: "low-medium to medium; friendliness and clarity above a fixed technique"
  allowed_hybrid:
    - "真实业务物件 + 轻量图形"
    - "平面/轻2.5D对象 + 简洁信息结构"
    - "生活化业务场景 + 必要标注"
```

Use:
- clean shapes;
- friendly visual hierarchy;
- simple, easy-to-scan business expressions;
- appropriate visual warmth.

Do not force:
- a step-card structure on every topic;
- 2.5D objects on every page;
- sticker-like reminders as a permanent signature.

Avoid:
- heavy audit style;
- dark serious corporate dashboards;
- advanced financial-report aesthetics.

## 4. Graphic Language（图形语言）

```yaml
graphic_language:
  relationship_lines: "简洁、柔和、方向清楚；仅在关系需要解释时出现"
  arrows: "短而清晰，可圆角但不作为强制风格符号"
  cards: "可用轻量圆角卡，也可不用；卡片服务内容而不是决定内容"
  labels: "短、口语化、2–6字"
  icons: "简洁业务图标，可平面、轻2.5D或其他清晰形式"
  highlights: "珊瑚橙/桃红/浅青绿局部强调，避免满屏贴纸感"
  borders: "柔和浅边框，按当前版式需要使用"
  shadows: "轻柔阴影"
  depth: "轻量层次；可平面、轻2.5D或选择性真实物件"
```

Recurring business objects may include:
- 营业执照;
- 公司印章;
- 银行账户;
- 发票;
- 报税;
- 社保;
- 工资;
- 代理记账资料;
- 个体户 / 公司主体.

They are selected by content, not required to appear.

## 5. Composition（构图倾向）

```yaml
composition:
  preferred_layouts:
    - "单一问题 + 清楚重点"
    - "左右轻对比"
    - "对象聚焦 + 简短说明"
    - "内容确有顺序时使用轻流程"
    - "生活化业务场景或简洁信息页"
  whitespace: "较多留白"
  focal_strategy: "先看到用户当前最关心的问题、错误、动作或结论"
  symmetry: "偏平衡、稳定，也允许轻微非对称增强活力"
  perspective: "正视、轻2.5D、编辑式场景或真实物件均可"
  camera_language: "轻盈、友好、易读，不固定成办事说明书"
```

The visual should feel understandable at first glance, not expert-only.

## 6. Business Object Style（业务对象表现）

```yaml
business_objects:
  documents: "营业执照、合同、票据等可按内容采用真实感、简化卡片或局部示意"
  accounts: "账户问题需要时使用，不默认做成银行卡/账户卡"
  money_flow: "收付款关系需要时用简洁方向或金额表达"
  goods_flow: "经营场景需要时可自由使用"
  process_nodes: "仅在真正存在步骤时使用，不把所有内容做成流程卡"
  risk_markers: "珊瑚色提示、错号或其他轻量风险强调按语义选择"
  check_markers: "浅青绿完成/匹配语义按需要使用"
```

The current topic determines which objects appear and how realistic or schematic they should be.

## 7. Human Presence（人物使用）

```yaml
human_presence:
  default_use: "supporting_or_selective"
  maximum_role: "通常不超过画面信息权重的25%，除非人物动作是当前内容的重要语义"
  preferred_view: "局部人物、手部、侧身、完整人物或简化人物均可按内容选择"
  preferred_action: "创业、办事、拿资料、操作账户、经营等真正帮助理解的动作"
  avoid:
    - "甜妹自拍感"
    - "无信息价值的双人职场对话"
    - "夸张搞笑表情"
    - "网络主播动作"
    - "商务握手"
```

Human warmth is allowed. Do not force people into every page and do not remove them solely to preserve a card-based look.

---

## 8. Information Density（信息密度）

```yaml
information_density:
  level: "low-medium"
  max_primary_relations: 1
  max_process_steps: 4
  max_labels: "3–5个"
  visual_scan_target: "1 second"
```

Keep information light enough for:
- first-time founders;
- individual businesses;
- small-company owners.

---

## 9. Text Usage（图内文字）

```yaml
text_usage:
  chinese_text_level: "low-medium"
  short_labels: "允许步骤名、对象名、短提醒和口语化结论"
  numbers: "只保留真正有帮助的金额/时间/数量"
  typography_feel: "轻量、友好、清晰、年轻但专业"
```

Long instructions stay out of the image. Do not make every page resemble a tutorial card set.

## 10. Preferred Visual Forms（优先示意形式）

```yaml
preferred_visual_forms:
  - "object_focus"
  - "comparison"
  - "process_when_order_is_real"
  - "relationship"
  - "risk_highlight"
  - "reconciliation"
  - "real_scene_support"
  - "simple_status_or_number_focus"
```

Selection rule:
- choose the form that makes the current business issue easiest for a first-time entrepreneur to understand;
- no form is a permanent account template;
- 2.5D, step cards, and stickers are optional techniques only.

## 15. Completion Check（完成检查）

- First-time founders can understand the image quickly.
- The result feels friendly, modern, and professional without becoming childish.
- It does not default to step cards, 2.5D objects, or sticker layouts.
- It does not resemble a dark data dashboard or senior consulting report.
- The image shows the current issue, action, or conclusion clearly.

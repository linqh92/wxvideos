# Account Visual Style（账号视觉风格）｜退税小笔记

> Account ID: `tsxbj`
>
> This file defines the long-term visual DNA for spoken supporting visuals.
>
> 本文件只定义“这个账号怎么看起来”，不定义“这条内容讲什么”。
>
> The account source does not define a fixed gender, age, or years of experience. Do not invent them visually or narratively.

---

## 1. Visual Positioning（视觉定位）

```yaml
visual_identity:
  core_impression: "亲和服务型出口退税视觉：专业、清楚、有完成度，但不高冷，让客户感觉复杂问题可以被一步步讲明白"
  audience_feeling: "像一个专业又好沟通的出口退税服务者，知道客户为什么着急，也能把订单、票、货、钱和退税问题讲得清楚"
  differentiation_goal: "与翩翩的冷静判断气质分开；小笔记更亲和、更有服务感、更强调易理解和持续信任，但不绑定工作笔记或文档拼贴形式"
```

Visual principle:

> **先让客户看懂并感到问题可被理清，再用专业完成度建立信任。**

Account Visual DNA controls warmth, clarity, information rhythm, and finish. It does not require paper, notes, highlighters, document collage, cards, or dashboards as permanent motifs.

---

## 2. Rendering Language（渲染语言）

```yaml
rendering:
  primary: "professional business information visual with approachable editorial clarity and high PPT-level finish"
  secondary: "clean 2D information design, selective business objects, real-object support, schematic relationships, or light interface modules when useful"
  realism_level: "content-adaptive; professional clarity and service warmth above a fixed rendering technique"
  allowed_hybrid:
    - "专业信息设计 + 当前业务对象"
    - "真实物件或场景 + 简洁解释层"
    - "关系、对比、流程、状态、数字或文件按当前内容选择"
```

The style should feel:
- professional;
- helpful;
- organized;
- approachable;
- polished without feeling cold or overengineered.

Historical production direction supports a high-completion business PPT feel with clear hierarchy, generous whitespace, restrained technology cues, and professional information modules. These are quality characteristics, not fixed dashboard requirements.

## 3. Color System（色彩系统）

```yaml
color:
  primary_colors:
    - "冷白"
    - "浅灰蓝"
  secondary_colors:
    - "深蓝灰"
    - "浅蓝"
  accent_colors:
    - "商务橙"
    - "柔和暖橙"
  warning_colors:
    - "有限红色，仅用于真实异常或高风险状态"
  background_preference: "冷白到浅灰蓝的明亮浅色背景，保持充足留白"
  saturation: "整体中低饱和，强调色中等"
  contrast: "中等，重点局部提高"
```

Color meaning:
- cool white / light gray-blue = clean professional baseline;
- dark blue-gray = credibility and information hierarchy;
- light blue = secondary support;
- business orange = key conclusion, action, money, or risk emphasis.

The palette should feel professional and approachable rather than governmental, cold, or decorative.

## 4. Graphic Language（图形语言）

```yaml
graphic_language:
  relationship_lines: "简洁、清楚，仅在业务关系需要解释时使用"
  arrows: "方向明确、克制，不做手写批注式固定语言"
  cards: "可使用白色或浅色商务卡片，但不把卡片作为固定主视觉"
  labels: "短、清楚、偏客户能理解的业务语言"
  icons: "简洁专业业务图标，可线性、几何或其他统一形式"
  highlights: "商务橙用于关键词、结论、关键金额或风险；不依赖荧光笔和圈重点"
  borders: "浅蓝或冷灰细边按需使用"
  shadows: "轻柔、克制"
  depth: "清晰2D为主，可按内容加入轻微层次或真实物件"
```

Recurring business vocabulary may include:
- 订单;
- 采购;
- 发票;
- 报关;
- 物流;
- 收汇;
- 供应商;
- 退税款.

These are content objects, not fixed visual motifs.

## 5. Composition（构图倾向）

```yaml
composition:
  preferred_layouts:
    - "单一问题 + 清楚重点"
    - "左右或前后对比"
    - "对象/场景 + 必要说明"
    - "内容需要时使用关系、流程、状态或数字结构"
    - "高完成度信息页 + 充足留白"
  whitespace: "较多"
  focal_strategy: "第一眼先看到客户最需要理解的问题、关键点、结果或下一步"
  symmetry: "偏稳定、友好，允许轻微非对称提升自然感"
  perspective: "正视、轻透视、编辑式物件或真实场景均可按内容决定"
  camera_language: "专业商务、清楚、服务感强；不固定为工作笔记或系统Dashboard"
```

Avoid cluttered collage, scrapbook, or overly technical interface aesthetics.

## 6. Business Object Style（业务对象表现）

```yaml
business_objects:
  documents: "订单、合同、票据、报关等需要时可真实感、简化或局部表现，不固定为纸张拼贴"
  accounts: "账户或收款主体影响判断时使用，可采用卡片、数字、场景或其他直观形式"
  money_flow: "需要时用清楚方向、金额或业务关系表达，不固定为批注线"
  goods_flow: "货物、物流、集装箱等内容需要时可作为主视觉或辅助对象"
  process_nodes: "真实流程存在时使用，不把所有问题强行拆成便签节点"
  risk_markers: "商务橙或有限红色突出真实风险，不依赖圈点符号"
  check_markers: "匹配、完成、核对等语义按当前页面选择最清楚的表达"
```

The current business meaning determines which object appears and whether it should be realistic, schematic, editorial, or modular.

## 7. Human Presence（人物使用）

```yaml
human_presence:
  default_use: "selective"
  maximum_role: "通常不超过画面信息权重的15%，除非人物动作对当前业务理解不可替代"
  preferred_view: "手部、局部人物、侧身、完整人物或真实业务动作均可按内容选择"
  preferred_action: "核对、沟通、处理资料、经营、收付款或其他真正帮助理解的动作"
  avoid:
    - "固定性别人物形象"
    - "固定年龄人物形象"
    - "无信息价值的双人对话"
    - "商务肖像摆拍"
    - "夸张表情"
```

Because the account has no fixed biography, avoid visually inventing a persona. Human warmth may come from interaction and visual tone rather than a recurring fictional character.

## 8. Information Density（信息密度）

```yaml
information_density:
  level: "low-medium"
  max_primary_relations: 1
```

The user should feel guided, not audited. High completion does not mean high density.

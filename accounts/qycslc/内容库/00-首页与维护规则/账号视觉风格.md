# Account Visual Style（账号视觉风格）｜企业财税-老陈

> Account ID: `qycslc`
>
> This file defines the long-term visual DNA for spoken supporting visuals.
>
> 本文件只定义“这个账号怎么看起来”，不定义“这条内容讲什么”。

---

## 1. Visual Positioning（视觉定位）

```yaml
visual_identity:
  core_impression: "稳重全局的企业决策视觉：老练、克制、有长期视角，重要事情看得不只是一眼"
  audience_feeling: "像一个资深顾问在关键决策旁边，把眼前选择和后续影响讲清楚，但不把每个问题都做成复杂结构图"
  differentiation_goal: "区别于基础创业教程、成熟经营诊断和快速状态判断；视觉更稳、更安静、更有全局与长期感"
```

Visual principle:

> **先看清当前决定，再保留对未来影响的视野。**

The account should feel:
- strategic;
- mature;
- calm;
- high-trust;
- long-term without requiring a fixed diagram language.

Account Visual DNA defines how decisions feel, not whether the page must contain an equity tree, timeline, or consulting diagram.

---

## 2. Rendering Language（渲染语言）

```yaml
rendering:
  primary: "premium mature business visual with content-adaptive editorial, schematic, object, or restrained realistic rendering"
  secondary: "professional information design, selective business objects, or simple scene support when semantically useful"
  realism_level: "medium-low to medium; structure and trust above a fixed technique"
  allowed_hybrid:
    - "企业关系 + 必要业务对象"
    - "比例/数字 + 简洁信息结构"
    - "真实场景或文件 + 克制解释层"
```

The visual should feel:
- strategic;
- mature;
- calm;
- high-trust;
- board-level in judgment, not in literal boardroom imagery.

Do not force:
- an equity tree on every equity topic;
- a timeline whenever future consequences are mentioned;
- consulting-report diagrams as the only professional expression.

## 4. Graphic Language（图形语言）

```yaml
graphic_language:
  relationship_lines: "稳定、克制、少量；仅在关系需要解释时出现"
  arrows: "少量、方向明确，不作为固定结构"
  cards: "矩形或轻圆角专业卡片可选，不固定为咨询卡模板"
  labels: "短、正式、少量"
  icons: "极简几何或业务图标，成熟、低噪音"
  highlights: "沙金/铜金用于价值、比例、关键决定或核心重点"
  borders: "细线、低对比"
  shadows: "非常轻"
  depth: "以视觉秩序和成熟层次为主，可平面也可选择性真实"
```

Common business vocabulary may include:
- 股东;
- 公司;
- 控制权;
- 股权比例;
- 融资;
- 核心员工;
- 退出;
- 股权转让;
- 资金与税;
- 时间变化.

These terms do not prescribe a mandatory diagram or node structure.

## 5. Composition（构图倾向）

```yaml
composition:
  preferred_layouts:
    - "单一关键决定 + 必要后果"
    - "前后/左右状态对比"
    - "比例或利益焦点"
    - "当前内容需要时使用关系、时间、场景或对象"
    - "稳定大留白的专业信息页"
  whitespace: "较多"
  focal_strategy: "核心决定、比例、利益关系或后续影响作为唯一主焦点"
  symmetry: "偏稳定、平衡"
  perspective: "正视、平面、编辑式物件或克制真实场景均可"
  camera_language: "成熟、稳重、咨询级完成度，但不固定为咨询结构图"
```

## 6. Business Object Style（业务对象表现）

```yaml
business_objects:
  documents: "股东协议、章程、交易文件等按内容选择真实感、简化卡片或局部表达"
  accounts: "仅在资金与股权判断相关时出现，可自由选择表现形式"
  money_flow: "仅在资金关系影响当前结论时使用"
  goods_flow: "内容需要时可使用，不做账号级禁止"
  process_nodes: "仅在真正存在阶段、进入、退出或变化顺序时使用"
  risk_markers: "暗铜红用于真实关键风险，不固定为断裂节点"
  check_markers: "沙金/深绿用于确认或稳定状态，按语义使用"
```

The business problem determines the object and structure. The account style should not force nodes, trees, or timelines where they are unnecessary.

## 7. Human Presence（人物使用）

```yaml
human_presence:
  default_use: "rare_or_selective"
  maximum_role: "通常不超过画面信息权重的15%，除非人物关系本身是当前决策语义"
  preferred_view: "手部、背影、局部、完整人物或真实互动均可按内容需要选择"
  preferred_action: "签署、讨论、决策、出资、加入或退出等真正帮助理解的动作"
  avoid:
    - "无信息价值的多人会议"
    - "泛商务握手"
    - "成功人士肖像作为权威替代品"
    - "夸张冲突"
    - "年轻互联网创业鸡血画面"
```

The account's authority should come from judgment and visual restraint, not elite-person imagery.

---

## 8. Information Density（信息密度）

```yaml
information_density:
  level: "medium"
  max_primary_relations: 1
  max_process_steps: 5
  max_labels: "4–6个"
  visual_scan_target: "1 second"
```

Allow structural complexity only when the current decision genuinely requires it. Do not turn every topic into a full legal/equity diagram.

## 10. Preferred Visual Forms（优先示意形式）

```yaml
preferred_visual_forms:
  - "comparison"
  - "relationship"
  - "process_when_sequence_matters"
  - "object_focus"
  - "risk_highlight"
  - "reconciliation"
  - "real_scene_support"
  - "number_or_proportion_focus"
```

Selection rule:
- choose the visual form from the current decision problem;
- relationship trees, timelines, and consulting diagrams are optional methods, not the account identity;
- preserve calm, mature pacing regardless of the chosen form.

## 15. Completion Check（完成检查）

- The image feels calm, mature, and high-trust.
- The current decision and its meaningful consequence are easy to understand when relevant.
- The page does not rely on equity trees, timelines, or consulting diagrams merely to look strategic.
- It does not look like a basic registration tutorial or a fast risk-alert dashboard.
- Authority comes from judgment, hierarchy, proportion, and restraint rather than elite-person imagery.

# Text-Broadcast Reading and Editing

This reference contains medium-specific editing guidance for silent, line-by-line WeChat Video Account text. The main Skill owns acquisition judgment and information selection; use this file after the editorial direction is established.

## Reading Capacity

Apply priorities in this order:

1. user-specified length or reference length;
2. explicit current-account length requirements;
3. the defaults below.

For an ordinary piece, aim for approximately 90–130 Chinese characters and 10–16 lines. Extend toward 160 characters only when an accurate conclusion cannot survive further narrowing; do not default beyond 200 characters.

These ranges are editorial baselines, not targets to fill. A shorter or differently divided piece is acceptable when it delivers the complete judgment more effectively. If the material is too large, narrow the question instead of compressing several conclusions into one piece.

## Information Capacity

Keep:

- one core conclusion;
- one primary pressure point or benefit point;
- no more than three explicitly developed judgment conditions;
- no more than one professional concept requiring explanation;
- one useful result, risk or action direction.

Secondary conditions may be compressed. Never hide a condition that changes the applicable audience, conclusion, risk level or recommended action.

## First-Screen Editing

Use no more than four lines before establishing the value of the piece. The opening may lead with a supported result, conclusion, benefit, loss, number, operating conflict, comparison or decision question.

Rewrite when the opening contains only greetings, background, emotion, generic questions, course-like setup or phrases such as `很多老板不知道`, `这个问题需要注意`, `今天来说一下` or `最近有人问` without immediate high-value information.

A scene may create relevance, but it must not postpone the actual issue merely to manufacture immersion.

## Line Composition

- Put one core information unit on each line.
- Prefer approximately 7–15 Chinese characters per line when meaning permits.
- Let important numbers, results and judgments stand alone when that improves recognition.
- Break at changes in condition, result or logic.
- Check whether a sentence longer than 30 Chinese characters should be divided.
- Use 2–4 lines per information block as a rhythm reference.
- Avoid more than two consecutive explanatory blocks.
- Keep the ending within three lines when possible.
- Do not break a complete meaning merely to create short lines.
- Never hand post-production a solid paragraph that still needs structural editing.

Use numbering only when parallel items, steps or checks genuinely become easier to scan. Do not force a fixed-size list.

## Available Editorial Shapes

The following are optional ways to understand an information relationship, not templates to select or fill:

- result and reason;
- mistaken decision and corrected standard;
- accumulation and amplified consequence;
- upstream problem and downstream effect;
- numeric or decision comparison;
- verified event and customer impact;
- scene, decisive variable and action direction.

Choose the actual reading path from the topic, audience and account role. Do not invent a change, trend, result or audience misconception to fit a shape.

## Reduction Pass

After the first complete draft:

1. remove repeated conclusions;
2. remove rule background and definition-style explanation that do not change the decision;
3. remove conditions that do not define the current conclusion;
4. remove transition-only sentences;
5. remove repeated results and repeated calls to action;
6. replace abstract professional language with a concrete customer consequence;
7. when the body remains too long, narrow the scope rather than shortening every sentence mechanically.

The final piece must not read like a long article, report, training outline or spoken script with arbitrary line breaks.

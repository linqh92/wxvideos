# Acquisition and Fact Framing

This file defines the shared decision rules for acquisition strength, claim framing, source use, and verification across topic planning, copywriting, and acquisition-cover planning.

## Objective

Operate inside legal, platform, business, and non-fabrication boundaries while maximizing:

- target-customer recognition;
- opening retention;
- numeric and outcome contrast;
- pain-point relevance;
- perceived risk or benefit;
- professional trust;
- consultation and service-conversion readiness.

Core directives:

> Compliance boundaries determine what the content may claim. Acquisition objectives determine how strongly permitted content should be expressed.

> Classify the information role first, determine verification second, then use the strongest expression allowed by that role and source.

Accuracy requirements primarily govern determinative facts and professional conclusions. Discussion claims, hypothetical scenarios, and rhetorical framing should remain strong when their role is clear.

## Information Roles

### Determinative Fact

Treat a claim as a determinative fact when any of the following applies:

- it identifies a person, company, location, date, authority, document, or event;
- it is introduced as a verified case, official notice, confirmed finding, or final enforcement outcome;
- it is used as evidence for a policy meaning, penalty outcome, eligibility condition, or professional conclusion;
- a reasonable viewer would understand it as current, confirmed, and factually endorsed by the content.

Current policies, tax rates, deadlines, penalty standards, platform rules, local rules, and processing conditions require current official verification when used as determinative facts.

### Discussion Claim

Treat a claim as a discussion claim when all of the following apply:

- it comes from user-provided material, authorized project material, identifiable public discussion, or an external reference;
- it does not identify a subject, location, date, authority, or case document;
- the surrounding language marks it as something being discussed or circulated;
- its function is to introduce a pain point, conflict, or analysis rather than prove the final conclusion.

Chinese oral cues such as `你是不是也听说过`, `最近圈里在讨论`, and `网上有这样一种说法` can frame the adjacent preceding statement as part of the same discussion unit. Evaluate the complete opening unit instead of isolating one sentence and upgrading it to a determinative case.

A discussion claim may retain specific numbers, outcome words, risk tension, and audience callouts. The body must provide an independent professional judgment. Do not expand the discussion claim into an officially confirmed case or generalize its outcome to every similar business.

Operational example:

> 1900万的流水拆成五个执照，结果被罚了400万。做电商的老板们，你是不是也听说过？

Classify the two sentences together as a discussion claim. The second sentence establishes hearsay framing for the opening unit. The body must explain the actual business facts and conditions that determine the professional conclusion.

### Hypothetical Scenario

Treat a scenario as hypothetical when it is marked with natural conditional language such as `如果`, `假设`, or `比如`.

The model may create hypotheticals from current rules, reproducible calculations, or ordinary business relationships. It must not present a hypothetical as a real client, real enforcement case, or official outcome.

Specific amounts may be used for calculation, contrast, or impact demonstration when the relationship is reproducible from stated assumptions or clearly identified as illustrative.

### Professional Conclusion

Treat an answer about whether something will happen, can be done, should be done, qualifies, or may create a consequence as a professional conclusion.

A professional conclusion must:

- follow current applicable rules;
- preserve every condition that changes the answer;
- distinguish possibility, normal expectation, legal obligation, and certainty;
- remain independent from any discussion claim used in the hook;
- avoid guarantees about processing, refunds, compliance, penalties, tax burden, or business results.

## Source Modes

### Reference Available

User material, authorized project content, and external references may supply discussion claims, anonymous cases, scenarios, numbers, risk conflicts, hook mechanics, and conversion devices.

When the user asks to follow a reference, preserve acquisition points, or stay close to a hook, retain or reconstruct the strongest useful functions:

- key numbers;
- outcome or loss;
- target-audience callout;
- cognitive conflict;
- suspense and reason to continue;
- CTA tied to an actual service need.

Adjust only the factual commitment that exceeds the claim's role. Lack of an official case source is not by itself a reason to turn a sourced discussion hook into a generic question, vague warning, or low-intensity setup.

### No Reference Available

The workflow may generate strong acquisition questions, business conflicts, hypotheticals, calculations, and outcome projections.

Specific material must come from at least one of these sources:

- a verified policy or public fact;
- material permitted by the current account content library;
- information supplied by the user in the current task;
- an explicitly framed, reasonable hypothetical or reproducible calculation.

Do not invent a precise amount, penalty outcome, client history, or case detail and then disguise it as discussion with language such as `你是不是也听说过`.

## Verification Decision

Apply this sequence:

1. Identify the role performed by the claim in the current content.
2. Identify its source.
3. Determine how a reasonable viewer will interpret it.
4. Determine whether it is being used to prove the final judgment.
5. Check whether it asserts a current policy, rate, deadline, penalty standard, platform rule, local rule, or processing condition.

Then act by role:

| Role | Required action |
| --- | --- |
| Determinative fact | Verify when current or consequential; state only the supported scope. |
| Discussion claim | Preserve discussion framing and acquisition value; do not use it as proof. |
| Hypothetical scenario | Keep assumptions visible and reasoning reproducible. |
| Professional conclusion | Verify the governing rule when needed and preserve conclusion-changing conditions. |

When a claim appears to mix roles, use the strongest factual commitment a reasonable viewer would perceive. Apply an adjacent discussion cue to the complete opening unit when the cue naturally scopes over the preceding statement.

## Acquisition Preservation

Use the strongest role-appropriate conflict, number, outcome, benefit, loss, or decision tension in the title and opening.

For every generated or rewritten piece, verify that:

- the strongest usable number and result remain visible;
- the target customer can immediately recognize relevance;
- risk, benefit, or loss reaches a concrete business outcome;
- the opening creates a clear reason to continue;
- a requested reference's acquisition function is preserved or rebuilt at equal usable strength;
- the CTA follows from the specific business need exposed by the content.

Use a short, natural role cue when clarification is necessary. Do not place a long disclaimer in the title, first screen, or spoken opening when words such as `听说`, `如果`, `可能`, or another natural cue can establish the role.

## Prohibited Uses

- Inventing a customer, case, amount, penalty, or outcome that viewers would understand as real.
- Presenting a discussion claim as an official notice or verified case.
- Presenting a hypothetical as personal experience.
- Turning an individual outcome into an inevitable result for all similar businesses.
- Fabricating authority, insider information, scarce access, or service capability.
- Hiding a condition that changes the professional conclusion.
- Replacing professional judgment with a guarantee or promised benefit.
- Removing acquisition value solely because a phrase contains an amount, penalty, or risk term.

## Stage Application

- `idea-intake`: preserve source material without adding factual endorsement.
- `topic-planning`: identify source, information role, acquisition angle, and necessary verification.
- `text-broadcast-copywriting`: place the strongest usable number, outcome, or conflict on the first screen.
- `spoken-copywriting`: preserve natural discussion framing, audience callout, outcome tension, and reason to continue.
- `spoken-visual-planning`: extract the strongest confirmed acquisition point without changing its information role or weakening it on the covers.
- `publish-archive`: record actual published content and state without adding factual endorsement.

## Quality Gate

Rewrite when any of the following applies:

- a discussion claim is treated as a determinative case;
- an adjacent discussion cue is ignored;
- a result-first hook becomes a generic question solely because an anonymous discussion claim lacks an official case source;
- a reference-provided number, loss, audience callout, suspense device, or service-relevant CTA disappears without a role-based reason;
- a precise generated case is disguised as discussion;
- a discussion claim becomes the only basis for a professional conclusion;
- a professional conclusion lacks a decisive condition or conflicts with a current rule;
- a disclaimer is more prominent than the acquisition point;
- the title, first screen, spoken opening, or acquisition cover fails to use the strongest angle allowed by the material and compliance boundary.

Final checks:

1. Is the information role clear?
2. Is the professional conclusion reliable?
3. Does the acquisition strength reach the maximum allowed by the source and compliance boundary?
